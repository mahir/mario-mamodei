# Library index

Study notes on conceptual artists, classic and contemporary, for Mario Mamodei's runs. Each file covers who the artist is, key works with checked titles and years, their core moves, the ideas worth keeping, what Mario could learn, what he must not reuse, and sources.

Read this index and [MOVES.md](MOVES.md) first. Then pick two or three files that bear on the idea you are forming. Facts marked [checked] or [known] may be cited; check anything marked [verify] before citing it.

## Precursors and classic Conceptual art, US and UK

- [Art & Language](art-and-language.md). British collective, founded 1968 in Coventry, England, with a New York branch in the 1970s. The most theory-driven group of Conceptual art. Moves: conversation as work, index, hidden content.
- [Douglas Huebler](douglas-huebler.md). American, 1924 to 1997. Worked in Massachusetts, New York and Los Angeles, where he taught at CalArts from 1976 to 1988. A founding figure of Conceptual art. Moves: duration, location, system plus camera.
- [Ian Wilson](ian-wilson.md). British, born in South Africa, 1940 to 2020. Born in Durban, moved to the United States in 1960, lived in New York from 1966 to 1986. Made spoken conversation his only medium. Moves: oral communication, no residue, certificate as trace.
- [John Baldessari](john-baldessari.md). American, 1931 to 2020. Worked in Southern California, taught at CalArts. Brought humour, photography and everyday language into Conceptual art. Moves: delegation, destruction as new start, punishment as form.
- [John Cage](john-cage.md). American, 1912 to 1992. Composer, writer and teacher in New York and at Black Mountain College. Precursor of Fluxus and Conceptual art. Moves: chance, the frame, score as text.
- [Joseph Kosuth](joseph-kosuth.md). American, born 1945. Based in New York and Europe. Leading theorist of Conceptual art as an inquiry into language and definition. Moves: definition as work, tautology, substitution.
- [Lawrence Weiner](lawrence-weiner.md). American, 1942 to 2021. Born in the Bronx, worked in New York and Amsterdam. Made language itself the material of sculpture. Moves: language as object, optional making, the receiver decides.
- [Lucy R. Lippard](lucy-r-lippard.md). American, born 1937 in New York City. Critic, curator, writer and activist; the chronicler of Conceptual art. Moves: chronology as argument, the card catalogue, population as title.
- [Marcel Duchamp](marcel-duchamp.md). French-born American, 1887 to 1968. Worked in Paris and New York. The main precursor of Conceptual art. Moves: selection, signature as act, the sealed secret.
- [Mel Bochner](mel-bochner.md). American, 1940 to 2025. Born in Pittsburgh, worked in New York. Early Conceptual artist of measurement, documents and language. Moves: the copy as exhibition, measurement, language as surface.
- [Robert Barry](robert-barry.md). American, born 1936. Based in New York and New Jersey; taught at Hunter College from 1964 to 1979. Pioneer of invisible and immaterial Conceptual art. Moves: invisibility, absence, release.
- [Sol LeWitt](sol-lewitt.md). American, 1928 to 2007. Central figure of Minimal and Conceptual art in New York. Moves: instruction, system, transfer of authorship.

## Fluxus, time, systems and institutional critique

- [Adrian Piper](adrian-piper.md). American, born 1948 in New York. Lives in Berlin. Conceptual artist and analytic philosopher (PhD Harvard 1981) whose work moves from abstract systems to race, identity and the ethics of everyday encounters. Moves: catalysis, self as material, persona.
- [Alison Knowles](alison-knowles.md). American, 1933 to 2025. Founding member of Fluxus in New York, known for event scores about food, daily routine and chance, and for an early computer poem. Moves: instruction, routine as found art, gift or exchange.
- [Bas Jan Ader](bas-jan-ader.md). Dutch, born 19 April 1942 in Winschoten, the Netherlands; lost at sea in 1975, aged 33. Worked in Los Angeles and the Netherlands. Conceptual artist of falling, failure and feeling. Moves: gravity as subject, failure on purpose, deadpan sincerity.
- [Felix Gonzalez-Torres](felix-gonzalez-torres.md). American, born in Cuba, 1957 to 1996. Worked in New York. Used ordinary materials (clocks, candy, paper, light bulbs, billboards) to make quiet, open works about love, loss and the AIDS crisis. Moves: gift or exchange, endless supply, portrait by substitution.
- [George Brecht](george-brecht.md). American, 1926 to 2008. Trained research chemist who became a founding figure of Fluxus and the inventor of the "event score." Moves: instruction, found moment, loose format.
- [Hanne Darboven](hanne-darboven.md). German, 1941 to 2009. Born in Munich, raised and based in Hamburg; worked in New York 1966 to 1968. Made vast handwritten works out of calendar dates, numbers, copied texts and images. Moves: the rule and its record, writing without meaning, inventory.
- [Hans Haacke](hans-haacke.md). German, born 1936 in Cologne. Lives in New York. Moved from art about physical and biological systems to art about the social systems of money and power, and became a founder of institutional critique. Moves: real-time system, institutional critique, public record as material.
- [Jenny Holzer](jenny-holzer.md). American, born 1950 in Gallipolis, Ohio. Works in New York State. Uses text in public places, on posters, LED signs, stone benches and light projections, to make language itself the artwork. Moves: language as object, anonymous voice, contradiction by accumulation.
- [Marcel Broodthaers](marcel-broodthaers.md). Belgian, 1924 to 1976. Born in Brussels, died in Cologne on his 52nd birthday; also lived in Düsseldorf, Berlin and London. Poet turned artist, author of a fictional museum and a founder of institutional critique. Moves: fiction as institution, misuse of an official form, language as object.
- [Mieko Shiomi](mieko-shiomi.md). Japanese, born 1938 in Okayama (as Chieko Shiomi). Composer and Fluxus artist based in Osaka; spent 1964 to 1965 with Fluxus in New York. Moves: circulation, simultaneity at a distance, mapping.
- [On Kawara](on-kawara.md). Japanese, born December 1932 in Kariya, Japan (official date 2 January 1933), died 2014 in New York. Lived in New York for about fifty years; a central figure of conceptual art built on time and daily record. Moves: the rule and its record, duration, daily proof.
- [Yoko Ono](yoko-ono.md). Japanese-born, born 1933 in Tokyo. Worked in Tokyo, New York and London. Pioneer of the instruction piece, close to Fluxus. Moves: instruction, impossibility, participation.

## Latin America, Eastern Europe, the Soviet Union and Asia

- [Cildo Meireles](cildo-meireles.md). Brazilian, born 1948 in Rio de Janeiro, where he still lives and works. Conceptual artist who began working under Brazil's military dictatorship (1964 to 1985). Moves: insertion, circulation, misuse of an official form.
- [Collective Actions and Andrei Monastyrski](collective-actions.md). Russian group founded in Moscow in March 1976, led by Andrei Monastyrski (born 1949 as Andrei Sumnin). Central to Moscow Conceptualism; still active. Moves: empty action, absence, certification.
- [Hélio Oiticica](helio-oiticica.md). Brazilian, 1937 to 1980. Rio de Janeiro artist who moved from Neo-Concrete painting to wearable capes, walk-in environments and immersive slide works. Showed in London in 1969 and lived in New York for much of the 1970s before returning to Rio. Moves: color as event, wearing the work, environment.
- [Hi-Red Center](hi-red-center.md). Japanese collective, Tokyo, active 1963 to 1964 (Wikipedia says founded May 1963), by Jirō Takamatsu (1936 to 1998), Genpei Akasegawa (1937 to 2014) and Natsuyuki Nakanishi (born 1935; widely reported to have died in 2016, not confirmed here). Moves: mimicry of civic order, misuse of an official form, interference in daily routine.
- [Ilya Kabakov](ilya-kabakov.md). Soviet-born, 1933 (Dnipropetrovsk, now Dnipro, Ukraine) to 2023. Unofficial artist in Moscow until 1987, then based in the West. After his move west, works are credited jointly with Emilia Kabakov (born 1945). Moves: fictional author, total installation, trace of an absent person.
- [Luis Camnitzer](luis-camnitzer.md). Uruguayan, born 1937 in Lübeck, Germany, raised in Uruguay, based in New York since the 1960s. Artist, teacher and writer, and the main voice arguing that Latin American conceptualism is its own tradition. Moves: language as object, labeling the world, the gap between word and image.
- [Lygia Clark](lygia-clark.md). Brazilian, 1920 to 1988. Neo-Concrete artist in Rio de Janeiro who moved from painting to objects handled by the public, then to body-based therapy sessions. Lived in Paris from 1968 into the 1970s. Moves: participation, the act as the work, choice inside a rule.
- [Marta Minujín](marta-minujin.md). Argentine, born 1943 in Buenos Aires, where she lives and works. Pioneer of happenings, immersive environments and huge public monuments made to be taken apart. Moves: happening, destruction as making, monument as gift.
- [Mladen Stilinović](mladen-stilinovic.md). Yugoslav and Croatian, 1947 (born in Belgrade) to 2016 (died in Pula). Worked in Zagreb as a key figure of the New Art Practice in socialist Yugoslavia. Moves: idleness as work, language as object, exploiting dead signs.
- [Tehching Hsieh](tehching-hsieh.md). Taiwanese-American, born 1950 in Taiwan. Arrived in New York in the 1970s after jumping ship at age 24, and made his major works there while living without legal papers. Known for year-long performances. Moves: duration, the rule and its record, endurance.
- [Tucumán Arde](tucuman-arde.md). Argentine collective action, 1968, in Rosario and Buenos Aires, by artists of the avant-garde group from Rosario working with journalists, sociologists and a trade union. Includes Graciela Carnevale's locked-room action of the same year. Moves: counter-information, overinformation, changing the venue.
- [Xu Bing](xu-bing.md). Chinese, born 1955 (the Met gives Sichuan Province; Art21 gives Chongqing, then part of Sichuan), grew up in Beijing. Trained as a printmaker at the Central Academy of Fine Arts. Lived in New York for nearly two decades after 1989, now based in Beijing. Moves: illegible authority, language as object, hybrid system.

## Contemporary artists working with ideas

- [Ceal Floyer](ceal-floyer.md). British, born 1968 in Karachi, Pakistan; died December 2025. Lived and worked in Berlin. Made very spare works in which an ordinary object, sound or word is shifted just enough to become a joke, a puzzle and a thought. Moves: literalism, representation in place of the thing, sound standing in for an event.
- [David Horvitz](david-horvitz.md). American, born 1982 in Los Angeles, where he lives and works. Makes small, often instruction-like works about time, distance, the sea, and how images and ideas spread online. Moves: instruction as gift, circulation, misuse of an open platform.
- [Dora García](dora-garcia.md). Spanish, born 1965 in Valladolid. Studied in Salamanca and at the Rijksakademie, Amsterdam; long based in Brussels. Makes performances and texts that slip into real time and real places. Moves: live description, insertion, stealing as reception.
- [Francis Alÿs](francis-alys.md). Belgian, born 1959 in Antwerp. Trained as an architect; moved to Mexico City in the mid 1980s and has made most of his work there. Known for walks and simple actions that become fables. Moves: the walk as drawing, futile labour, rumour or hearsay.
- [Hito Steyerl](hito-steyerl.md). German, born 1966. Filmmaker, artist and essayist based in Berlin. Writes and films about how images circulate, how they are made by machines, and who pays for them. Moves: circulation, the essay as artwork, deadpan instruction.
- [Jill Magid](jill-magid.md). American, born 1973 in Bridgeport, Connecticut. Lives and works in New York. Works from inside institutions of power (police, secret services, corporate archives) by following their own rules. Moves: misuse of an official form, seduction of the system, commission as trap.
- [Jonathan Monk](jonathan-monk.md). British, born 1969 in Leicester. Studied at Leicester Polytechnic and Glasgow School of Art; lives and works in Berlin. Remakes the classics of Conceptual and Minimal art with humour and his own family history. Moves: appropriation, inversion, completing the system.
- [Pierre Huyghe](pierre-huyghe.md). French, born 1962. Works with fiction, rituals and living systems (animals, plants, bacteria, machines) that keep changing whether or not anyone watches. Moves: transfer of authorship, invented ritual, living sculpture.
- [Roman Ondák](roman-ondak.md). Slovak, born 1966. Lives and works in Bratislava. Makes quiet interventions that slip ordinary behaviour into exhibition spaces. Moves: staged ordinariness, the empty queue, accumulation.
- [Ryan Gander](ryan-gander.md). British, born 1976. Makes works built from loose associations, invented artists, near-invisible gestures and stories the viewer must finish. Moves: loose association, invented artists, the withheld work.
- [Sophie Calle](sophie-calle.md). French, born 1953. Works in Paris. Makes works of photographs and text from rules she sets for her own life: following, hiring herself out, collecting strangers' traces. Moves: self-set rule, following, inventory of traces.
- [Tino Sehgal](tino-sehgal.md). British-born artist, born 1976 in London, raised in France and Germany, based in Berlin. Trained in political economy and dance; makes "constructed situations" made only of live human encounter. Moves: withdrawal of the object, the oral contract, interpreters as medium.
- [Trevor Paglen](trevor-paglen.md). American, born 1974. Artist and geographer who photographs secret places and studies how machines see. Works between photography, research and engineering. Moves: seeing the unseen, distance as content, amateur infrastructure.

## Machines, data, networks and the smallest gesture

- [Cory Arcangel](cory-arcangel.md). American, born 1978 in Buffalo, New York. Long based in Brooklyn, now in Stavanger, Norway. A programmer-artist who hacks games, software and online culture. Moves: subtraction hack, the default tool, found crowd.
- [Harold Cohen](harold-cohen.md). British-born, worked in the United States, 1928 to 2016. An established London painter who moved to the University of California, San Diego, in the late 1960s and spent over forty years writing AARON, one of the first art-making computer programs. Moves: knowledge as code, the lifelong program, live machine drawing.
- [Holly Herndon and Mat Dryhurst](holly-herndon-and-mat-dryhurst.md). Holly Herndon (American, born 1980) and Mat Dryhurst (British, born 1984). Based in Berlin. Musicians and artists who work with machine learning as a medium and argue for consent and shared ownership in AI. Moves: train your own, identity as a public instrument, governance as form.
- [Ian Cheng](ian-cheng.md). American, born 1984 in Los Angeles. Lives and works in New York. Studied cognitive science and worked at the effects studio Industrial Light & Magic before making "live simulations" with video game engines. Moves: the self-running world, infinite duration, narrative versus ecology.
- [JODI](jodi.md). Artist duo formed in 1994: Joan Heemskerk (Dutch, born 1968) and Dirk Paesmans (Belgian, born 1965 in Brussels). Based in the Netherlands. Pioneers of net art in the mid-1990s. Moves: error as image, show the source, mod and strip.
- [Katie Paterson](katie-paterson.md). Scottish, born 1981 in Glasgow. Lives in Fife and works in Edinburgh and London. Makes simple, exact works that connect a human scale to geology, ice, the moon and deep time. Moves: transmission and loss, a line to the remote, lifetime units.
- [Lauren Lee McCarthy](lauren-lee-mccarthy.md). American, born in Boston. Based in Los Angeles, where she teaches at UCLA. Performance artist and programmer; creator of the open-source coding platform p5.js. Moves: becoming the machine, consensual surveillance, service as artwork.
- [Martin Creed](martin-creed.md). British, born 1968 in Wakefield, England. Studied at the Slade, London (1986 to 1990). Turner Prize winner 2001; a leading figure of minimal, deadpan conceptual art. Moves: the numbered list, title as instruction, almost nothing.
- [Rirkrit Tiravanija](rirkrit-tiravanija.md). Thai, born 1961 in Buenos Aires, the son of a Thai diplomat; raised in Thailand, Ethiopia and Canada. Divides his time between New York, Berlin and Chiang Mai. Known for turning galleries into kitchens and social spaces. Moves: gift or exchange, hospitality as form, swap front and back.
- [Ryoji Ikeda](ryoji-ikeda.md). Japanese, born 1966 in Gifu, Japan. Lives and works in Paris (some sources add Kyoto). Composer and visual artist who turns raw data, sound and light into large, precise installations. Moves: data as landscape, overload past reading, universal conversion.
- [Song Dong](song-dong.md). Chinese, born 1966 in Beijing, where he lives and works. A leading figure of Chinese conceptual art since the 1990s, working in performance, photography, video and installation. Moves: trace-less mark, daily practice, breath as material.
- [Vera Molnár](vera-molnar.md). Hungarian-born French artist, 1924 to 2023. Born Vera Gács in Budapest, settled in Paris after 1948. A pioneer of computer-generated art and of rule-based geometric drawing. Moves: the small disorder, imagined machine, variation by tiny steps.
